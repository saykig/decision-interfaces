## Exact paper claim and dependency chain

For nonempty compact convex uncertainty families of strictly positive independent
Bernoulli parameters below the receiver threshold, preserve all declared fixed-order
strategic questions with positive sender costs/rewards and favorable equilibrium
ties. The exact reusable component is the downward family, equivalently its full
positive weighted-log support profile. Approximation is measured in contextual
incentive-margin units; independent occurrences add directed errors. Finite label
masks remain visible, and bounded reuse controls representation accuracy.

For fixed rational box constants `0<ell<u<tau<1`, planar convex sources inside
`[ell,u]^2`, and the all-order/all-positive-rational-cost contract requiring answers
on every query with absolute log margin at least γ, the finite-budget representation
rate is matching Θ(γ^−1/2), with source-readable rational certification. The declared
local budget is strictly smaller than the final separated margin; at most k
independent repeats changes the per-code rate to Θ(√(k/γ)). Merely assuming each
input is strictly positive would not give a uniform finite bit bound over inputs
approaching zero.
Unlimited reuse rules out fixed finite worst-case bits at a fixed final margin.
Generic geometric entropy is credited to established work. These are the inherited
R15/R16 written statements under their exact domains, not new entropy theorems.
